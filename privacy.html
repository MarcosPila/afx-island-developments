Privacy

Your information, in plain words

This website doesn't track you. It has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. If you ask for an estimate, you send it yourself from your own phone or email.

Last updated 26 November 2026

Who we are

AFX Island Developments does home renovations and repairs in the GTA and surrounding areas. You can reach us at afxdevelopments@gmail.com or 416 435 9967.

What this website collects

Nothing. The site has no sign-in, no contact form that sends data to a server, no advertising, no analytics and no cookies. It doesn't load anything from other companies' servers: even the fonts come from our own site.

The free estimate builder

The estimate builder writes a message for you right in your browser. Nothing leaves your device while you fill it in. When you finish, you choose Send by text or Send by email, and your own messages or email app opens with the message ready to send. We only receive it if you press send there.

If you send it, we receive what you chose to include, such as your name, project description, phone number, email address, area and any photos you attach. We use it to reply to you, plan a visit and give you an estimate. Your text or email provider handles the message on its way to us, under its own privacy policy.

What is stored in your browser

The site remembers two small things on your own device. We never see them.

  • afx-theme: your light or dark choice, if you use the switch. It stays until you clear your browser data.
  • afx-cta: whether you closed the floating estimate bar. It clears when you close the tab.

Website hosting

Like any website, the company that hosts these pages may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads them. We don't use that information. This site is currently hosted on GitHub Pages, and we'll update this page if that changes.

Your choices

If you've sent us a request and want us to correct it or delete it, email afxdevelopments@gmail.com and we'll take care of it. We don't sell or share your information.
