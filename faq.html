FAQ

Questions

Straight answers. If yours isn’t here, send a text or give us a call.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Tell us about your project by text, email or the estimate form and we’ll get back to you with a free estimate. There’s no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

The GTA and the surrounding areas. If you’re just outside, ask anyway.

How big a job do you take on?

Anything from small repairs to larger renovations: interior and exterior work, carpentry and framing, flooring and tile, painting, demolition, outdoor projects and general maintenance.

Can I send photos of the space?

Please do. When your message opens after you tap Send by text or Send by email, attach a few photos before you send it. They help us give you a more accurate estimate.

Do you handle demolition and clean-up?

Yes. Tear-outs, debris removal and site prep are part of what we do, so the new work starts on a clean slate.
