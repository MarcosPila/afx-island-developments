About AFX Island Developments

An owner-run renovation company serving the GTA.

AFX Island Developments is Alberto Fernandez’s renovation company. The AFX in the name comes from his initials, and it goes on every job.

Whether it’s a small repair or a larger renovation, the approach is the same: listen first, give you a clear estimate, do the work properly and leave the place clean.
